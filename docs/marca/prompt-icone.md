# Prompt para gerar o ícone do Barrio

Cole isso no gerador de imagem:

---

Design a modern, professional app icon for "Barrio", a bar and restaurant
management software. The icon must be a single, simple, iconic illustration
of a beer keg (barril de chopp) — clean geometric style, flat design with
subtle depth (soft gradients and shadows, not photorealistic, not 3D render,
not clipart).

**Composition:** centered, front-facing beer keg silhouette, filling most of
a square canvas with generous even padding on all sides. Rounded square
("squircle") background, not a perfect circle and not a hard-cornered square.

**Color palette:** background is a warm copper/burnt-orange gradient,
roughly from #d97a3f (lighter, top-left) to #96491c (darker, bottom-right).
The keg illustration itself uses a warm off-white/cream (#fdf0e8) as the
main fill, with darker copper tones (#7a3d18 or similar) for shading,
outlines, and the metal hoops/bands of the keg. Keep the palette to these
2-3 tones total — no other colors.

**Style references:** think of modern SaaS app icons (Linear, Notion,
Slack) — bold, simple, geometric, confident, a little playful but not
cartoonish. NOT a generic beer-mug clipart icon, NOT a realistic stock-photo
style keg, NOT overly detailed (it needs to read clearly at 32x32px, like a
browser favicon).

**Avoid:** text/lettering of any kind, photorealism, busy detail, drop
shadows outside the icon, multiple objects/props (no glasses, no foam, no
bar scene) — just the keg itself as a clean confident mark.

**Format:** square image, 1024x1024, transparent or single flat background
(no separate scene behind it) — ready to be placed directly as an app icon.

---
